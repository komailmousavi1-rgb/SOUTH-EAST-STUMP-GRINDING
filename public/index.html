Before you book

Good to know.

What is stump grinding?

A grinder chips away the visible stump and grinds below ground level. The scope and depth depend on the site and what you plan to do with the area.

What helps you quote?

Share your suburb, the number of stumps and an approximate width. Mention narrow gates, steps or anything close to the stump that could affect access.

What happens to the wood chips?

Grinding leaves wood chips and soil. If you’d like the chips left for mulch or removed, mention that in your enquiry so we can confirm what’s included in the quote.

Do you cover my suburb?

We service South East Melbourne. Include your suburb in your quote request so we can confirm coverage for your address.

Tell us where it is and we’ll be in touch about a quote.
